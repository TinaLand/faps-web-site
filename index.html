FAPS

Failure-Aware Policy Shielding for Frozen Vision-Language-Action Policies

FAPS detects when SmolVLA is entering a risky state and injects a low-rank activation residual into the action expert, aiming to recover without fine-tuning the base policy.

View Results Poster PDF Experiment Summary

Project Summary

A safety layer that acts inside the policy, not after it.

Most robot policy shields intervene after an action is already produced. FAPS instead reads intermediate SmolVLA activations, predicts near-term failure risk, and edits a hidden state before downstream transformer layers finish producing the action.

Phase 1 Risk is detectable.

BCE-based risk heads reach about 0.927 weighted AUC on windowed perturbation episodes.

Phase 2 Residuals are learnable.

C12/C13 flow runs produce substantial nonzero corrections in the PCA activation space.

Phase 3 The bottleneck is triggering.

The current diagnostic shows false-positive injections are the main issue before final shield eval.

Method

Detect, gate, and correct at inference time.

The base SmolVLA policy stays frozen. FAPS adds a detector, a hysteresis trigger, and a flow-matching residual model that edits one action-expert layer.

01

Encode risk

A 3.76M-parameter risk head reads SmolVLA activations, actions, proprioception, and short temporal context to produce a risk score and embedding z_t.

02

Trigger carefully

A hysteresis state machine enters shielding only after high risk persists, then exits after risk stays low for K steps.

03

Inject a residual

Flow matching predicts a low-dimensional correction. A PCA basis maps it back into the hidden activation space of the selected layer.

Phase 3 Status

Closed-loop recovery is the final test.

The current online shield result is a diagnostic, not the final claim. It shows that false-positive triggering can hurt recovery, especially when the detector fires on otherwise safe steps.

The next evaluation uses a nominal-aware retrained risk head, then tests B5/C12/C13 candidates with the same threshold settings.

Main lesson so far

When the shield avoids false-positive injections, perturbed shielded episodes look promising. When it triggers at the wrong time, it can over-correct and lower success.

Next Steps

What remains before a strong paper claim.

Retrain the trigger

Train the risk head with nominal-success episodes as clean negatives, then rerun calibration and threshold sweep.

Run Phase 3 ladder

Start with B5 layer-11 smoke, then threshold smoke, then compare B5, C12, and C13 under the same settings.

Report closed-loop success

The final headline should be success improvement, recovery rate among triggered episodes, and nominal false-alarm control.
